What it does

One surface. Many possibilities.

Floor & Wall turns a selected SketchUp surface into an editable procedural system instead of a manually modeled collection of pieces.

01 / PATTERNS

Procedural layouts

Generate architectural patterns without modeling each piece by hand.

02 / GEOMETRY

Real pieces

Thickness, bevel and fitted geometry produce a usable modeled result.

03 / CLIPPING

Respect boundaries

Fit generated pieces to surface edges and openings.

04 / MATERIALS

Material workflow

Work with library, scene and imported materials inside the same process.

05 / EDITING

Regenerate

Adjust parameters and rebuild the system instead of starting again.

06 / WORKFLOW

Four guided steps

Select a pattern, configure it, choose materials and generate.

Why is it free?

An introduction to Structura.

Floor & Wall is free because it is the most direct way to experience how Structura tools work before purchasing another product.

This is not a reduced demo tier or a timed trial. The objective is to make the Structura workflow accessible and let the product prove the system.
