Guided workflow

From surface to generated pattern.

The UI direction is deliberately visual and step-based rather than a dense technical panel.

1. Choose pattern

Browse large pattern thumbnails, categories and search to choose the base surface logic.

2. Configure geometry

Adjust dimensions, bevel, thickness, clipping and pattern-specific parameters.

3. Choose material

Work with Structura Library, Scene materials or Imported textures through a consistent source-aware workflow.

4. Review & generate

Review pieces, complexity and generation state before creating the final procedural surface.

Material variation

Use controlled physical or source-aware variation to reduce visible repetition without damaging the source material.

ArchViz production

Designed around real SketchUp rendering workflows, with V-Ray-aware development and attention to stable UVs.
