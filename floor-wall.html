How Floor & Wall works

One surface. Many possibilities.

Create floors and wall coverings directly inside SketchUp with a fast, visual and parametric workflow.

01 — Select the surface

Select the surface

Choose the floor or wall you want to transform directly inside SketchUp.

Start from any compatible face.
02 — Choose a pattern

Choose a pattern

Pick from a library of wood, tile, stone and architectural patterns. From simple planks to more complex layouts, the geometry is generated from the pattern you choose.

One surface. Many possibilities.
03 — Make it yours

Make it yours

Adjust dimensions, spacing, thickness, bevel and other pattern settings. See how the design adapts before generating the final geometry.

Control the look, not the modelling process.
04 — Choose the material

Choose the material

Use a Structura material, select one from your SketchUp scene or import your own texture. Add subtle variations when needed to create a more natural result.

Your pattern. Your material.
05 — Generate

Generate

Floor & Wall builds the complete pattern directly on the selected surface. Pieces, borders and clipping are resolved automatically to fit the geometry.

Select. Customize. Generate.
