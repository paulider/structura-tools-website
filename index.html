Why Road Click

The difficult part is not drawing a road. It is making it work on real terrain.

Road Click is designed around the situations that make road modeling slow in SketchUp: uneven ground, continuous slopes, intersections, sidewalks and repeated road markings. The goal is to turn that complexity into a direct visual workflow.

CORE DIFFERENTIATOR / TERRAIN

Draw the route. Let Road Click solve the road against the terrain.

Instead of flattening the whole context or rebuilding the ground manually, Road Click is built to generate road geometry in terrain with relief and adapt the terrain around the road system. This makes it useful for real architectural and urban-context scenes, not only flat diagrams.

INTERSECTIONS

Crossings as part of the network

Connected road segments are treated as a system, allowing intersections and junction conditions to be resolved coherently.

ROAD MARKINGS

Detail without redrawing

Generate lane lines, crossings and compatible road markings as part of the same workflow.
