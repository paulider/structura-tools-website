Created in production

Software built inside real ArchViz studios.

Structura Tools is created by Pau G.Cols, founder of RenderBcn and Studio Render Italia. After more than 15 years producing architectural visualizations for architects, developers and design studios, Structura was created to solve the recurring SketchUp workflows that cost time in real projects.

This production background is not a side story. It is the reason the tools exist and the standard against which they are developed.

Why Structura

Professional tools, not generic utilities.

The design principle is simple: identify repetitive production problems and turn them into focused systems that remain understandable inside SketchUp.

01

Production driven

Features begin with recurring real-project problems rather than speculative software concepts.

02

SketchUp native

Workflows are designed around how architectural teams actually model, edit and iterate.

03

ArchViz oriented

Geometry, materials and downstream rendering are considered from the start.

04

System architecture

Each plugin is useful alone, but the long-term direction is a coordinated Structura ecosystem.
