Why Road Click

Real roads rarely live on flat terrain.

The hard part of road modeling in SketchUp starts when the site has slopes, changing elevations and connected junctions. Road Click is designed to simplify exactly that: draw the route, generate the road, adapt the terrain, resolve intersections and add road markings in one connected workflow.
