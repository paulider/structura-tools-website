Why Road Click

Road modeling should not take hours.

Traditional road workflows in SketchUp are repetitive: offsetting, trimming, rebuilding intersections, placing markings and repeating the same operations. Road Click turns that process into a connected procedural workflow.

NETWORK

Connected roads

Build a road network rather than isolated pieces of geometry.

INTERSECTIONS

Automatic nodes

Resolve connected road segments and intersection conditions as part of the system.

ROAD TYPES

Configurable streets

Control widths, lanes, sidewalks and road typologies before generation.

MARKINGS

Road logic

Add lines, crossings and direction-dependent markings without drawing them one by one.

URBAN ASSETS

Infrastructure

Extend the road system with compatible lights, signals and urban elements.

PRODUCTION

Built for real scenes

Designed for architectural visualization and urban context workflows inside SketchUp.
