Structura Roads · Active development

Build complete road networks directly inside SketchUp.

Structura Roads is being developed as a connected procedural system rather than a collection of isolated road pieces. The goal is coherent generation across centerlines, editable nodes, profiles, junctions, terrain and downstream ArchViz workflows.

System scope

A road network, not a road command.

The public positioning should reflect what Roads is becoming without pretending every development module is already final or released.

Point-to-point creation

Create road centerlines interactively, with snapping and inference-driven editing.

Existing centerlines

Use existing traces as the basis for a procedural road network.

Nodes & segments

Maintain a graph-based relationship between editable road segments.

Junctions & roundabouts

Develop intersections as network topology rather than disconnected geometry patches.

Profiles & markings

Support configurable road profiles, sidewalks, lanes and road-marking systems.

Terrain integration

Coordinate road generation with cut/fill, slopes and terrain reconstruction workflows.

Urban & mountain contexts

Handle different road environments without collapsing everything into one generic profile.

Incremental regeneration

Regenerate affected parts of the network instead of rebuilding the entire scene.

Diagnostics first

Extensive technical logging and reproducible build diagnostics are part of the development architecture.

Development philosophy

Architecture before patches.

Roads is being developed around processing order, shared topology, robust snapping, terrain-aware geometry and explicit diagnostics so improvements remain coherent as the system grows.
