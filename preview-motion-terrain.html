THE ENGINE / 01–04

THE HARD PART,
MADE SIMPLE.

Road geometry on an irregular site demands more than drawing a centerline. The workflow needs to understand the road, its context and the network around it.

Discover the workflow ↗
01 / DRAW

Click the route.

Start with a few points in the SketchUp scene. Focus on where the road should go rather than manually building each part.

02 / ADAPT

Work with the terrain.

Road Click is designed to address the road-terrain relationship on uneven, sloped sites and reduce manual ground editing.

03 / CONNECT

Resolve junctions.

Connected segments are treated as a road network, with intersection logic incorporated into generation.

04 / DETAIL

Finish the street.

Road markings, crossings and compatible urban elements turn paths into a complete street system.
