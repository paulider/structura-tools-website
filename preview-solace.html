(03) / CAPABILITIES

THE TOOL.

FOUR REASONS TO
USE ROAD CLICK

Road Click is being developed to create road geometry over sloped terrain and adapt the surrounding ground, reducing the need to model the terrain-road transition manually.

Road segments form a network. Connected nodes and crossings can be resolved within the generation workflow instead of constructed individually.

Lane lines, crossings and direction-dependent markings are generated as part of the street system, reducing repetitive drafting and alignment.

Extend roads with sidewalks and compatible urban elements such as lighting and signals, keeping the scene-building workflow together.
