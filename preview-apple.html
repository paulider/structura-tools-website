Road Click brings complex road construction into a clearer, more direct SketchUp workflow. The focus is on the elements that normally take the longest to coordinate.

01 / Terrain

Made for real ground.

Designed to work with slopes, elevation changes and terrain adaptation rather than assuming every scene is flat.

02 / Junctions

Every road connects.

Create connected road networks, with intersections and crossings treated as part of a coherent system.

03 / Markings

Details, without the repetition.

Bring in road lines, crossings and compatible urban elements as part of your street-building workflow.
